PRE-REGISTRATION, paper v3 (written 2026-10-21, BEFORE any v3 run; no v3 DS2 result exists at time of writing)

Goal: test whether label-free, causal, per-patient class-prior (label-shift) correction improves inter-patient
heartbeat classification of the fixed 924-parameter CNN+RR model (architecture/width/recipe frozen from v2 CV).

Calibration (fitted on the patient-wise validation records only, never on scored records):
  Bias-corrected temperature scaling (BCTS, Alexandari et al. 2020): q = softmax(z/T + b), (T, b) minimise NLL
  on the validation beats. Source prior pi_s = class frequencies of those validation beats.

Adaptation methods (all label-free, evaluated per record, then pooled):
  M0  argmax of the raw softmax (v2 baseline)
  M1  BCTS only (no prior correction)
  M2  streaming EM (proposed): pi_0 = pi_s; for beat t, p_t = normalise(q_t * pi_{t-1} / pi_s); decision = argmax p_t;
      then pi_t = (1-lam) pi_{t-1} + lam p_t, floored at 1e-4 and renormalised. Uses only past and current beats.
  M3  offline EM per record (Saerens et al. 2002, uses the whole unlabeled record; non-causal reference)
  M4  oracle prior (true per-record class frequencies; upper bound, uses labels; reference only)

Choice of lam (the only free parameter), on DS1 only:
  4 folds (the v2 folds). For held-out fold k: validation (checkpoint + BCTS) = fold (k+1) mod 4; train = other two folds.
  Seeds 42 and 1 -> 8 cells. Score per cell = mean(F1_S, F1_V) on fold k, pooled over its records, method M2.
  Candidates lam in {1/50, 1/100, 1/200, 1/400, 1/800}.
  Rule: take the best mean score; choose the SMALLEST lam (slowest adaptation) whose mean is within one SE of the best.
  Adaptation is adopted as the primary result only if M2 with the chosen lam beats M0 in paired DS1 CV by more than one
  paired SE; otherwise it is reported as a negative result and M0 remains primary.

Real-time front end (secondary, also frozen before running):
  causal 4th-order Butterworth 0.5-45 Hz (sosfilt, one pass) + causal 60 Hz notch (Q=30) [MIT-BIH is US-recorded];
  causal Pan-Tompkins-style R-peak detector; a detection matches an annotation if within 150 ms (AAMI EC57).
  Beat labels come from the matched annotation; unmatched detections are counted as false detections and excluded
  from classification metrics; missed beats are reported and counted as misses in end-to-end sensitivity.
  Model retrained on DS1 with this front end (same recipe, 5 seeds 42,1,2,3,4), lam carried over unchanged.

Split-variance study (secondary): 20 random patient-wise splits of the 44 non-paced records (22 train incl. 5 val / 22 test),
  split seeds 0..19, one training seed each, front end = annotation R-peaks + original v2 preprocessing.
  Reported: distribution of test S/V metrics for M0 and M2; fraction of splits where M2 beats M0.

OUTCOME OF THE PRE-REGISTERED DS1 CV (appended 2026-10-21, before any DS2 adaptation result was computed or viewed):
  M0 0.524+-0.038 | M1 (BCTS only) 0.460 | M2 best lam=1/50 0.509; rule-chosen lam=1/800 0.496 | M3 0.521 | M4 oracle 0.614.
  M2 does not beat M0 by > 1 paired SE (diff -0.029, SE 0.036, 3/8 wins) -> pre-registered method NOT adopted.
  Diagnosis: argmax of calibrated posteriors (M1) loses the minority-favouring decision bias that class-weighted training
  builds into the raw softmax; M2 inherits that loss.

AMENDMENT A1 (post hoc, written after the DS1 CV outcome above, before any DS2 adaptation result):
  "Cost-preserving" variants: the prior pi_t is estimated exactly as in M2/M3/M4 (EM on BCTS-calibrated posteriors), but the
  decision is argmax_k softmax(z)_k * pi_t,k / pi_s,k, i.e. the raw model's decision rule with only the label-shift ratio
  applied (reduces exactly to M0 when pi_t = pi_s).  Named M2c (streaming, causal; uses pi_{t-1}), M3c (offline EM), M4c (oracle).
  lam for M2c chosen on the same DS1 CV cells with the same rule (smallest lam within 1 SE of best), same adoption criterion
  (beat M0 by > 1 paired SE).  Because A1 was designed after seeing DS1 CV results, the paper will label it as post hoc and
  report both the failed pre-registered M2 and A1 on DS2.

OUTCOME OF A1 ON DS1 CV: M2c best/chosen lam=1/800, 0.497, diff vs M0 -0.027 (SE 0.020), 3/8 wins -> NOT adopted.
  Cost-preserving oracle M4c 0.490 < M0. Calibrated oracle M4 0.614 remains the only variant above M0.
  Conclusion: no further label-free variants will be tried (to avoid a garden of forking paths). The paper is re-framed as an
  analysis of what limits S detection in tiny inter-patient models; M2 and M2c are reported on DS2 as negative results.

ADDENDUM A2 (quantification only, no selection; written before any DS2 adaptation result):
  M5k: per-record prior estimated from the LABELS of the first k minutes of the record (k = 2, 5), floored at 1e-4,
  decision = BCTS-calibrated posterior * pi_k / pi_s (as M4). Scored only on beats after minute k; M0 and M4 re-scored on the
  same beats for a paired comparison. Diagnostic D1: per-record |offline-EM S prior - true S prior| vs oracle.

OUTCOME OF A2 ON DS1 CV: M5 (first 2 or 5 min of labels) does not beat M0 (-0.028, 3/8 wins). Oracle M4 gain is driven by S PPV
  (0.37 -> 0.72) on S-free records; EM drives S prior to ~0 on S-free records (median 1e-4) but strongly under-estimates it on
  S-rich records (207: 0.058 true vs <=0.002; 209: 0.127 vs 0.011-0.031); corr(true, EM) = 0.30 for S vs 0.80 for V.

ADDENDUM A3 (written before any DS2 ensemble or adaptation result was viewed): seed ensembles, no tuning.
  E5 = mean of the softmax outputs of 5 independently trained 924-parameter models; two disjoint ensembles per front end:
  seeds {42,1,2,3,4} and {5,6,7,8,9}. Compared with the 10 single models. Also reported: E5 + M2 (pre-registered lam).

OUTCOME ON DS2 (9 of 10 seeds available, seed 42 pending): M2 0.707 vs M0 0.716 (not better); M2c, M3 worse; oracle M4 0.844;
  M5 (2 or 5 min labels) 0.848/0.853 vs M0 0.710/0.704 on the same late beats, 9/9 wins - opposite sign to DS1 CV (-0.028).
ADDENDUM A4 (written before any random-split result was computed or viewed): because the M5 result differs in sign between
  DS1 CV and DS2, M5_2 and M5_5 are added to the 20-split analysis (paired with M0 on the same beats after minute k).
  This split-level result, not DS2, is what the paper will treat as evidence on whether few-label prior correction helps.
